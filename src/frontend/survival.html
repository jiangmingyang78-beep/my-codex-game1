        Biome guide
        Survival guide
        • Dropped items are picked up automatically within 3 tiles.
        • Hold left-click to gather plants, harvest crops, pick up items within 5 tiles, or demolish structures. Click to plant seeds, open doors, or use facilities.
        • Hold a material, select a recipe, and right-click an empty tile to build. Hold right-click and drag to build repeatedly. On touchscreens, tap an empty tile.
        • Right-click an empty tile with empty hands to till it. On touchscreens, press and hold the tile.
        • With the watering can equipped, hold left-click and drag over crops to water them. Refill at a well or pond. A restored well automatically refills nearby watering cans.
        • Hold seeds, then hold right-click and drag over empty tilled soil to sow continuously.
        • Feed wheat to chickens and sheep, then collect eggs and shear wool.
        • Bake bread by the campfire and watch for monsters after dark.
        • Gather wild plants by hand and feed native plants to wildlife. Hold left-click to mine a vein.
        • Build a research table with 3 Wood and 1 Stone, then approach it and press T to research technology or contribute to camp repairs.
